UNLEARNING

What happens when belief meets experience?

Unlearning is an ongoing series of conversations exploring what happens when belief meets experience. Beliefs can change, weaken, survive, conflict with other beliefs, or simply become harder to hold with certainty — and sometimes a person changes without ever consciously recognizing that they have. The project is interested in that complexity, not in collecting stories of people realizing they were wrong.
